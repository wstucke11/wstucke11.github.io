ABOUT THE PROJECT

Intelligence without the noise.

ADDY Intelligence is a research-driven market intelligence project. This public companion shows independently discovered markets and eligible published observations; the private trading Workspace stays private.

Our principles

Availability is not a recommendation. A signal is not a trade. Accuracy is not profit. Unknown or stale evidence is displayed as unknown—not as success.

Market data

The directory discovers public Kalshi Crypto15 series through GitHub Pages and checks current contract bid/ask quotes through a separate, public-only Cloudflare relay. These are contract prices, not cryptocurrency spot prices. A multi-asset series may have many open contracts; the card shows one representative contract only.

Frequently asked questions

What do BUY, DON'T BUY, PASS and NO SIGNAL mean?

BUY and DON'T BUY require published ADDY assessments; DON'T BUY does not mean SELL or SHORT. PASS means ADDY explicitly abstained after assessment. NO SIGNAL means no published assessment exists. No approved public signals have been published.

Does seeing an asset mean it can be traded automatically?

No. Listing a market does not enable trading, authorize an asset, or change private execution permissions.

How frequently does this page update?

When the public relay is healthy, the app checks Kalshi contract quotes about every 18 seconds, with roughly 10 seconds of edge caching. Quote ages and source coverage are shown. Live quotes stop appearing current after 30 seconds. If the relay fails, the site uses GitHub's approximately five-minute published directory, clearly marked as delayed. Neither source is a guaranteed real-time exchange feed, and missing or incomplete market responses are not treated as successful quotes.

How can I install the app?

Use the Install App button if your browser offers installation. On iPhone use Share → Add to Home Screen. This public website uses HTTPS and supports Add to Home Screen.
